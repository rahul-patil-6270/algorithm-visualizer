🏃 Jump Search Visualizer

📘 What is Jump Search?

Jump Search works by jumping ahead by fixed steps and then performing linear search within the identified block.

🧠 Algorithm:

step = √n
prev = 0
while arr[min(step, n)-1] < x:
    prev = step
    step += √n
Linear search from prev to step
