Sorting

Definition: Sorting is the process of arranging data in a particular format or order, usually ascending or descending.

Importance: It enhances the efficiency of searching and data organization in various applications.

Real-life use: Timetables, contact lists, leaderboards, and databases.

Bubble Sort

Simple comparison-based technique. Suitable for beginners.

Selection Sort

Divides array into sorted/unsorted and repeatedly selects the minimum.

Insertion Sort

Builds the final sorted array one item at a time.

Merge Sort

Divide-and-conquer approach, very efficient for large datasets.

Quick Sort

Highly efficient, in-place divide-and-conquer technique.
