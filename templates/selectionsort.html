Selection Sort

Definition: Selection Sort repeatedly finds the minimum element from the unsorted part and puts it at the beginning.

Importance: Simple to understand, suitable for small data sets, but inefficient for large lists (Time Complexity: O(n²)).

Real-life use: Good for teaching sorting concepts, used in embedded systems with very limited memory.

Algorithm (Pseudocode):

for i from 0 to n-1: minIndex = i for j from i+1 to n: if arr[j] < arr[minIndex]: minIndex = j swap arr[i] and arr[minIndex]
